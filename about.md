# Custom sounds

Replace any Geometry Dash sound with your own file.

1. On the main menu press the new green button (bottom menu).
2. Find the sound with the search box. Press the play button to hear it.
3. Press **Replace** and pick your own `.mp3`, `.ogg` or `.wav` file.
4. Press the red cross next to a replaced sound to restore the original.

Replaced sounds are highlighted in green. Your files are copied into the mod's save folder, so you can move or delete the originals.
